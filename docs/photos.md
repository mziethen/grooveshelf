# Personal record photos

Open a record, expand **Manage record**, and choose **Manage photos**. Select Cover, Back cover, Label or Matrix, optionally enter a caption, choose a file and press **Save photo**. Choosing a file alone saves nothing; closing the dialog before saving cancels the draft.

JPEG, PNG and still WebP images up to 5 MiB are accepted, with at most 24 megapixels and a maximum side of 8192 pixels. Images are fully decoded, orientation is corrected, resized to fit 2048 × 2048 pixels, and stored as JPEG without embedded camera/EXIF/location metadata. Transparency is flattened onto white. Originals are not retained. The service allows 12 photos per physical copy, at most 2 MiB per normalized image and 128 MiB of stored personal photo data for the collection. Failed uploads keep the draft available for retry and create no photo row. The service serializes image processing to limit memory pressure on the Pi.

Choose a thumbnail to inspect the photo and its caption. **Use as cover** selects it for this copy; **Use album cover** returns to the saved album/Discogs cover without deleting photos. Choosing a Discogs cover also switches back to provider artwork. Deleting a photo requires confirmation; deleting the selected personal cover restores the normal album-cover behavior. Removing the record deletes its photos as well. Copies sharing an album keep separate photos and cover preferences.

Personal covers are independent of provider freshness: they stay visible when expired Discogs data is hidden. Metadata refresh, edits, release linking and sync preserve photo ownership and the personal cover choice. Uploaded photos stay in the local database and are never submitted to Discogs or a recognition provider. Photo recognition remains separate work in #22.

Schema version 10 adds a copy-owned `photos` table with bounded JPEG blobs, metadata and one selected-cover flag per copy. Existing collections migrate automatically without changing records, inventory numbers, tags, history or settings. **Portable ZIP backup** includes all photos and the cover choice through the database snapshot. ZIP format version 1 accepts schema 9 and 10 archives; restoring a schema-9 archive into a new destination upgrades it to schema 10 with an empty photo collection. Restores validate image contents, dimensions, ownership and limits. JSON/CSV/offline HTML exports do not embed photo binaries: use ZIP for a complete backup.

The image processing uses the official [Pillow Image API](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/Image.html) and [ImageOps orientation handling](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/ImageOps.html). Verification covers corrupt/oversized uploads, EXIF stripping, orientation, quotas, copy isolation, expiration and refresh, deletion, restart persistence, archive round trips, schema-9 migration, browser retries, cover switching, confirmation and all responsive themes.
