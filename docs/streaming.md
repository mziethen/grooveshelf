# Streaming search links

Open a record's details and find **Find this album online**. **Search Spotify**, **Search YouTube** and **Search Qobuz** open a new tab with a search for the record's artist and album title. Results can include other releases or editions; these are search shortcuts, not verified album matches.

GrooveShelf does not require service credentials, load embedded players, or contact any of these services while displaying your collection. Clicking sends the artist and album title to the selected service; the link suppresses the GrooveShelf page's referrer. Availability, account requirements and playback depend on the service.

Opening a search does not log a play, start a listening session, assign an NFC tag or change a record. Use GrooveShelf's listening controls or NFC workflow to log plays.

Links use the current saved artist and title, including manual corrections. When provider metadata is unavailable or expired, links are hidden unless both artist and title are protected personal corrections. Empty artist or title values also hide the section. Metadata refreshes and record edits update the search terms automatically.

Qobuz opens its public catalog search through the regional storefront router. The destination may redirect to your regional Qobuz store; it is not a direct web-player or playback link.
